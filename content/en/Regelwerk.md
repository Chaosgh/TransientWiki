## **§1 – General**

*§1.1 Principles*
 - ***The rules can be supplemented or changed at any time***
 - The **TransientRealm team** reserves digital property rights and can take action at its own discretion.
 - This set of rules applies **cross-platform** to all TransientRealm servers (Minecraft, Discord, website, etc.).
 - All members and players undertake to comply with the currently valid version of the rules.
 - **Ignorance does not protect against punishment** - everyone is responsible for informing themselves about the rules.

*§1.2 Changes & Updates*
- The rules can be expanded, adjusted or changed **at any time without prior notice**.
- Changes will be announced in the Discord server.
- By continuing to use the TransientRealm services, you automatically accept the current version.

*§1.3 Team instructions*
- **Instructions from team members** must be followed **immediately**.
- Discussions about team decisions are allowed in private conversations or via tickets, **not in public chat**.
- Disrespectful behavior towards the team will **not be tolerated**.

*§1.4 Identity & Appearance*
- **Usernames, profile pictures, skins and status messages** must be free of offensive, discriminatory, sexual or extremist content.
- Names must not be misleading (e.g. impersonating team members).
- The team can **propose a name change** or impose a ban for violations.

*§1.5 Political & controversial content*
- **Political discussions, propaganda and ideological content** are **banned** on all platforms.
- This also includes polarizing topics such as religion, extremism or social conflicts.
- The goal is a **neutral and friendly environment** for all players.

*§1.6 Evasion of sanctions*
- **Bypassing bans** through secondary accounts, VPN or other means is **strictly prohibited** and will result in a **permanent ban on all accounts**.
- Asking other people to act on your behalf is also considered circumvention.

*§1.7 Fairness & Integrity*
- **Bug using, exploiting and misusing game mechanics** are prohibited.
- Any bugs or security gaps found must be **reported to the team immediately** (ticket system or direct message).
- The **deliberate concealment** of bugs can be viewed as complicity.

*§1.8 Respectful cooperation*
- **Insults, discrimination, bullying and hate speech** of any kind are prohibited.
- This applies to all forms of communication: chat, voice, signs, books, buildings, etc.
- **Provocation and toxic behavior** will also be sanctioned.

## **§2 – Discord Specific Rules**

*§2.1 Chat behavior*
- **Spam, flooding and meaningless messages** are prohibited.
- **Caps-Lock** should be used sparingly (single words for emphasis are okay).
- **Chatting in the wrong channels** disrupts clarity - use the designated areas.
- The **automatic chat filter** may not be bypassed using special characters, spaces or paraphrases.

*§2.2 Prohibited content*
- **NSFW content** (pornographic, sexually explicit, extremely violent) is **prohibited with no exceptions**.
- **Illegal content** (instructions on crimes, links to pirated copies, drugs, weapons, etc.) will result in **immediate exclusion**.
- **Harmful links** (phishing, viruses, shock content) are prohibited.
- **Advertising external servers, services or products** is prohibited without permission.

*§2.3 Voice Channel Rules*
- **Noise, soundboards, voice changers and excessive volume** are not permitted.
- **Recordings of conversations** are only permitted with the consent of everyone involved.

*§2.4 Mentions & Notifications*
- **Excessive pinging** of individuals is prohibited.
- Pings to the team should only be made for **urgent matters** - otherwise use the ticket system.

*§2.5 Gambling & Giveaways*
- **Gambling, casinos and betting** may not be advertised or organized.
- **Giveaways and events** are only permitted with prior approval from the team.

*§2.6 Recruitment & Recruitment*
- **Recruiting** members for other servers, projects or communities is prohibited.
- Friendly recommendations in one-on-one meetings are okay, mass recruiting is not.

## **§3 – Minecraft specific rules**

*§3.1 Griefing & Destruction*
- **Definition:** Griefing refers to the intentional damage, alteration or destruction of structures, items or resources of other players without their express consent.

	**Forbidden:**
	 - **Destroying, changing or defacing** other people's buildings (even partially).
	 - **Stealing items** from chests, furnaces, armor stands, item frames, etc.
	 - **Kill or release** strange animals and villagers.
	 - **Remove or move** redstone circuits, farms or machines.
	 - **Contaminating** landscapes (e.g. placing lava/water, leaving holes).
	 - **Block** doors, chests or entrances to other people's properties.
	 - **Appropriation** or renaming of someone else's property.
	 
	**Exceptions:**
	- Buildings in the **Resource World** (Unless these buildings are part of or are farms such as a Creeper Farm or a Villager Farm)
	- Properties on the plot world where an admin has ordered a community grief
	
	**Consequences:**
	- Griefing leads to **warning, temporary or permanent ban** depending on the severity.
	- **Repeat offenders** are generally permanently excluded.

*§3.2 Traps, PvP and Combat*

- *§3.2.1 Traps*
    - **Definition:** Traps are structures or mechanisms designed to harm, kill or steal items from players.
    
    **Generally prohibited are:**
    - **Traps on other people's properties** or in public areas.
    - **Hidden traps** (e.g. lava under carpets, TNT traps, pitfalls)
    
    **Traps are only permitted if:**
    - **Both parties** have expressly agreed (e.g. in PvP events or competitions).
    - It is **own property** and visitors are warned in advance (e.g. by signs).
     
    **In case of violations:**
     - Traps will be **removed**, stolen items will be **refunded**.
     - The person responsible receives a **warning or ban**.

- *§3.2.2 PvP (Player vs Player)*

	PvP is **only allowed under certain conditions** on TransientRealm:
	
	**Allowed:**
	
	- In **designated PvP zones** such as the Ender Dragon Fight.
	- With **mutual agreement** (e.g. duels, events, wars).
	- In **planned events** (tournaments, team battles, etc.).
	
	**Forbidden:**
	
	- **Spawn Killing:** Repeatedly kill players immediately after respawn.
	- **Unfair Tactics:** Exploiting bugs, logging out to escape fights.
	- **PvP without consent:** Attack players outside of PvP zones.
	- **Combat Logging:** Log out during combat to avoid death or loss.
	
	**End Crystals & Explosive Items:**
	
	- End Crystals are **only allowed in PvP zones and with consent**.
	- **TNT, Withers and other destructive items** may not be used for griefing.

	**End Fight:**

	- Once a month players can fight against the Ender Dragon to get the Dragon Egg - The following things are forbidden in the Enderdragon Fight;
	   - Place the Dragon Egg in the Enderchest while in combat
	   - Use bed bombs against players - They are only allowed against the dragon.
	   - Also Traps & End Crystals
	   - Combat logging

*§3.3 Hacks, Mods & Cheating*

- *§3.3.1 Allowed Clients & Mods*
	- A list of allowed clients & mods can be found on the [WIKI](https://wiki.transientrealm.de/#/wiki/Anderes/erlaubte%20mods). 
	
	**If you are unsure:** Ask in the ticket system before using a mod.
	
	**Consequences of Cheating:**
	
	- **First offense:** 30 day ban.
	- **Second offense:** 90 day ban.
	- **Third offense:** Permanent ban
	- **Serious cases** (e.g. server crash, data manipulation): Immediate permanent ban.

 *§3.4 Exploits & database manipulation*

**Strictly prohibited are:**

- **Duplication glitches**
- **Chunk Dupe, TNT Dupe, Rail Dupe**
- **Exploiting server bugs**
- **Database manipulation, plugin exploits**.
- **DDoS attacks, server overload**.

**Requirement to report:**

- Exploits found **must be reported immediately** (ticket or DM to admins).
- **Reward:** Players who report serious bugs can expect in-game rewards.

**Consequences:**

- **Permanent ban** if used intentionally.
- **Reset accounts** (items, progress).
- In severe cases: **Legal action**.

*§3.5 Multiaccounting*

**Multiaccounting refers to the use of multiple Minecraft accounts by the same person.**

**Forbidden:**

- Use of second accounts to **bypass bans**.
- **Unfair advantages** (e.g. double votes without ranking via multiaccounting, multiple event participation, AFK farming).
- **Manipulation of statistics** or rankings.

**Allowed (with prior approval):**

- Secondary accounts for **legitimate purposes** (e.g. content creation, inventory management).
- Requests by ticket – the team decides on a case-by-case basis.

**Consequences:**

- **All connected accounts will be blocked**.

 *§4 – Final provisions*

- **By using TransientRealm** you agree to these rules.
- **Ignorance** or **misunderstanding** do not protect against sanctions.
- If you have any questions or are unclear: **Use the ticket system** or ask team members.

**The TransientRealm team wishes all players a lot of fun and fair cooperation!**

**Good luck on TransientRealm!**
