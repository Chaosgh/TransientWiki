# FAQ

## What is TransientRealm?

TransientRealm is a German Minecraft server with Citybuild, jobs, quests, an economy, crates, cults and other custom game systems. This wiki explains its features in both German and English.

## Where is the best place to start?

With the [Beginner's Guide](/wiki/anfaenger-guide) and the [Getting Started](/wiki/anderes/einfuehrung) page. Then it's worth taking a look at [Jobs & Professions](/wiki/jobs/berufe) and [Citybuild](/wiki/anderes/citybuild).

## How do I make money on the server?

Mainly through jobs, quests, trading and the server's custom economy. The [Jobs System](/wiki/jobs/berufe) is the best place to begin.

## Is there a Minecraft Jobs system?

Yes. TransientRealm has an extensive job and profession system with classic gathering and combat jobs as well as specialized roles such as sigil mage, rune mechanic and researcher.

## Are there quests and progression systems?

Yes. [Daily Quests](/wiki/anderes/daily-quests), crates, job levels and group systems provide long-term progression.

## What role does Citybuild play?

Citybuild is the basis for your own plotting, building, storage, shops and trading. You can find out more about this at [Citybuild](/wiki/anderes/citybuild).

## Are there factions or group systems?

Yes. With [Cults](/wiki/kult/kulte) and [Factions](/wiki/kult/fraktionen) there are social systems for cooperation, economics and shared progression.

## Where can I find an overview of all the important features?

On the [Server Systems](/wiki/server-systeme) page you will find the best overall overview.
