A successful cult needs a clear hierarchy. The ranking system allows you to distribute tasks and maintain control.

## Standard ranks

Each cult starts with these ranks (from low to high):

The **Power** value determines how high a member appears in the internal cult member list — higher power = higher up.

| Rank | Description | power |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **assistant** | New members without special rights. | 20 |
| **Member** | Trusted players. | 40 |
| **Officer** | Can kick and promote members. | 60 |
| **Confidant** | Right hand of the ruler. | 80 |
| **Ruler** | The founder and leader. Has all rights. | 100 |

## Permissions overview

The following rights can be assigned to ranks:

* **Invite Members:** Allows `/kult invite`.
* **Kick members:** Allows `/kult kick`.
* **MOTD change:** Allows `/kult setmotd`.
* **Change tag color:** Allows changing the tag color in the menu.
* **Edit Description:** Allows changing the cult description.
* **Cult Upgrade:** Allows you to purchase level upgrades.
* **Manage Ranks:** Allows you to create and edit ranks.
* **Promote/Demotion:** Allows changing the ranks of other members (only below your own rank).

## Custom ranks

From **Level 2** you can create your own ranks!

1. Open the menu: `/kult` → Ranks.
2. Click “Create Rank”.
3. Choose a name and assign rights.
