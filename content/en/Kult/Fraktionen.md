<div class="md-banner">
    <h1 style="margin: 0;">Factions</h1>
</div>

From **cult level 10**, your cult chooses one of three factions. Each faction unlocks its own bonuses starting at level 10, which become stronger as the cult level increases. Unlocked faction bonuses remain active and apply to all members across the cult.

## The factions

### <span style="color: #ffaa00;">The Order</span>

* **Description:** A traditional alliance of paladins. Discipline, defense and fighting monsters.
* **Focus:** PvE combat, defense.
* **Ranks:** Novice → Friar → Knight → Grand Knight → Grand Master.

| Cult Level | Bonus |
| :---: | :--- |
| 10 | **Order Discipline** — +4% damage against monsters |
| 11 | **Armory I** — +3 items & +2 slots in the Order Shop |
| 12 | **Blessing of the Order** — +10% damage against undead |
| 13 | **Fortitude** — −4% incoming monster damage |
| 14 | **Armory II** — +3 items & +2 slots in the Order Shop |
| 15 | **High Order Banner** — +2% PvE damage against all mobs, −4% incoming damage |

### <span style="color: #55ff55;">The Merchants' Association</span>

* **Description:** Dealers and shopkeepers who rely on discounts and trading networks.
* **Focus:** Economy, Shop Discounts.
* **Ranks:** Errand Boy → Merchant → Factor → Guild Master → Merchant.

| Cult Level | Bonus |
| :---: | :--- |
| 10 | **Commercial Law** — −8% price on standard items in the Clan Shop |
| 11 | **Account inventory I** — +5 items & +4 slots in the shop |
| 12 | **Market feel** — additional −4% (total −12%) |
| 13 | **Trading Network** — +3 Merchant Shop Slots |
| 14 | **Special Account** — −15% on exclusive dealer items |
| 15 | **Grosskontor** — total −15% on standard items and −20% on dealer items |

### <span style="color: #ffcc55;">The Peasantry</span>

* **Description:** Farmers who rely on crop yields and Farm Job XP.
* **Focus:** Farming, Loot.
* **Ranks:** Servant → Farmer → Hofmeister → Eldest → Gutsvogt.

| Cult Level | Bonus |
| :---: | :--- |
| 10 | **Land Blessing** — +6% loot drops from Crops |
| 11 | **Apprenticeship** — +10% Farm Job XP |
| 12 | **Rich harvest** — additional +6% loot (total +12%) |
| 13 | **Diligence of the Fields** — additional +5% Job XP (total +15%) |
| 14 | **Pantry** — +2 exclusive shop slots, +5 available items |
| 15 | **Thanksgiving** — −10% price on food/supply items, +6% loot (total +18%) |

## Joining & Switching

Once your cult has reached **Level 10**, the ruler chooses a faction in the cult menu. This choice is **not final** — you can change factions later:

* **Switching Cost:** 25,000 Rubies
* **Cooldown:** 7 days between two changes

<div class="md-note">
    <strong>Note:</strong> Changing factions costs rubies and has a cooldown - so think carefully about your choice.
</div>
