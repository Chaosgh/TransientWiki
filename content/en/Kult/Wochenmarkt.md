# Weekly market

The weekly market is an exclusive trading opportunity for cults from **Level 5**. Here you can buy rare items with **Pounds**.

## How does it work?

* **Rotation:** The offer changes every week (real time).
* **Currency:** Payment is made in Pounds.
* **Access:** All members of a cult (level 5 and above) can use the market.

## Offer

The offer is random and can contain anything from rare building materials to magical items. Check back regularly so you don't miss any bargains!

**Command:** `/kult markt` (or via cult menu)
