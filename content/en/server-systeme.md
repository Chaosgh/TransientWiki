Citybuild is the basis for land, construction, shops and trade. Together with currencies, warps and player shops, this creates the everyday economy of the server.

## What makes TransientRealm special

TransientRealm combines classic Citybuild basics with systems more familiar from RPG and MMO servers:

- Jobs with specialization
- Server-specific refinements and magic
- Progress through quests and rewards
- social systems with cults and factions
- Multiple currencies with clear roles: Pounds, Ectoplasm and Rubies
