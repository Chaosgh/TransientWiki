The Chef is the cooking job: prepare dishes at **Cooking Stations** to earn Job XP and Pounds.

## How do you earn XP?

- Complete recipes at the **Cooking Stations** — each recipe gives its own amount of Job XP and Pounds.
- It's your own **custom cooking system** (not the normal oven): with your own ingredients, cooking tools and recipes.
