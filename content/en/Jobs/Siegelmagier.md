## The Sigil Altar

The **Sigil Altar** is used to create powerful sigils that permanently strengthen your equipment. You craft it yourself — you can find the recipe at `/rezepte`.

There are different types of **basic sigils** from which full-fledged sigils can be made. The process is always the same:

1. Collect the matching **Aura** — a rare drop from a specific activity.
2. Use it to create a **Base Sigil** on the normal **Crafting Table**.
3. Place your **Sigil Altar** and **Right Click** on it to open the GUI.
4. Place the **Base Sigil** in the slot provided (on the left).
5. Place the **Item you want to enchant** and any other ingredients into the ingredient slots.
6. If the combination matches a recipe, the **Result** will appear in the results slot — click on it to get it and earn Job XP.

You can see all the recipes that correspond to your job level using the **Recipe Book icon** in the Altar GUI. The **“Fill Recipe”** button automatically inserts all the required items from your inventory.

| Base Sigil | Aura | Where does the aura come from?
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Earth Sigil** | Earth Aura | Occasional drop in **Researcher** job |
| **Water Sigil** | Water Aura | Drop while **fishing** |
| **Poison Sigil** | Poison Aura | Drop from the **Labyrinth** |
| **Air Sigil** | Air Aura | *Coming Soon* |

## Available sigils from level 1

Already from **Level 1** you can create the following sigils with the basic sigils:

* **Soul Bound** - The item is not lost upon death and remains with the player.
* **Thread Strength** - Allows you to catch stronger and rarer fish.
* **Drag** – Increases the weight of fish caught.
* **Night Watcher** – Grants permanent night vision.
* **Replant** – Plants are automatically replanted after harvesting.
* **Block Tracking** - Displays on the pickaxe how many total blocks have been mined.
* **Wisdom** – Increases experience gains.
* **Smelting Touch** – Mined ores are smelted directly.
* **Reinforcement** – Increases the durability of the item.
* **Magnet** – Automatically puts dropped items into your inventory.

## Available sigils from level 2

* **Poison** - Poisons your opponent when you hit them.
* **Vein Mining** – Mine an entire vein of ores at once.
* **Freeze** - Freezes your opponent when you hit them.
* **Lava Walker** – Lets you walk across lava.

## Available sigils from level 3

* **Drill** – Lets you mine a 3x3 area at once.
* **Timber** – Lets you mine an entire tree at once.

## Available sigils from level 4

* **Vampire** - When you deal damage to an enemy, you are healed for a percentage of that damage.

## Available sigils from level 5

* **Gold Hook** – Lets you catch higher quality fish.
