# Rune Mechanic

Combine technology with magic through the art of runes. The rune mechanic forges powerful equipment at a special altar.

## The rune table

The **Rune Table** is a special workbench for the rune mechanic. You craft it yourself — you can find the recipe at `/rezepte`.

How to use it:

1. Place your **Rune Table**
2. **Right click** on the table → the GUI opens
3. Place the required **ingredients** in the 4×4 ingredient grid (16 slots)
4. If the ingredients are correct, the **result** will appear in the results slot on the right
5. Click on the result to take it out → you will get Job XP

You can add up to **16 different ingredients** at the same time.

## Recipes

You can find all available recipes with the command `/rezepte`.

- Recipes are filtered according to your **job level** — new recipes unlock with higher levels
- The **“Fill Recipe”** button automatically puts all the required ingredients from your inventory into the table

Recipes are managed by the server team and are subject to change with updates.

## Earn Job XP

You will receive Rune Mechanic Job XP each time you successfully craft a rune. The amount of XP depends on the specific recipe.

## Commands

| command | Function |
|---|---|
| `/job` or `/berufe` | Open job menu, view progress |
| `/rezepte` | View all Rune Table Recipes |
