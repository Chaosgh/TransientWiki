Explore the past and unearth forgotten artifacts. The rarer the find, the more valuable the reward.

## How does it work?

The researcher digs for artifacts using a special tool:

1. Craft the **Explorer's Tool** (a special shovel - recipe at `/rezepte`) and pick it up
2. Use it to mine **sand**, **red sand** or **gravel** (e.g. in deserts, on beaches or in caves)
3. Each time you mine, you will receive small job XP
4. With **2% chance** you will unearth an **artifact** instead of raw materials - with bonus XP depending on rarity

## Artifact categories

Artifacts belong to different **collection categories**. Try to find all pieces in a category:

| Category | Topic |
|---|---|
| past | Objects from the history of the world |
| Science | Tools and discoveries |
| Argus | Mysterious finds of unknown origin |
| Author | Written traditions and records |

## Earth Aura

As a researcher, you have a chance to get **Earth Aura** when digging. This is needed by the **Sigil Magician** to create an **Earth Sigil**. Store them or trade them with other players.

## Tips

- Large deposits of sand and gravel in the **resource world** offer the most opportunities - mining is allowed there
- Sand, red sand and gravel all count - you can also find plenty in caves and along rivers
- Rare artifacts appear randomly — patience is the key to success
