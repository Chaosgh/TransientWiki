The waters hold many secrets — from common fish to rare treasures.

## The fishing mini-game

Fishing on the server is not a simple matter of waiting: when a fish bites, a **minigame** starts automatically.

A progress bar with a **green target zone** is displayed in the title. A marker automatically moves to the right. Your task:

- Press **right click** to drag the marker towards the target zone
- Hold the marker **in the target zone** until the hold timer expires
- You have a total of about **20 seconds** - then the fish escapes

The action bar shows you: holding time, progress in %, and the total time remaining.

## Difficulty levels

Each catch has a randomly determined difficulty. Rare fish are harder to catch:

| Difficulty | Opportunity | Target zone | drift |
|---|---|---|---|
| Very light | ~20% | Large | Low |
| Easy | ~40% | Medium | Low |
| Medium | ~30% | Medium | Medium |
| Hard | ~8% | Small | High |
| Very Hard | ~2% | Very small | Very high |

## Fish quality & weight

Each fish caught has two random attributes:

**Quality** (affects sales value):
| Quality | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Tough | ~30% |
| Edible | ~26% |
| Tasty | ~22% |
| Delicate | ~13% |
| Gourmet | ~9% |

**Weight** (in kg) — also greatly influences the sales value. The heavier, the more Pounds.

## Biomes & Regions

Different fish can only be caught in certain **biomes** or regions. There are different fish in the Citybuild world than in the farming world. Some rare fish also require a fishing rod with the **Rodpower** enchantment.

The exact locations of the fish are **intentionally not documented** - discovering them is part of the gaming experience. Use the `/anglerbuch` to see which species you haven't caught yet.

## The fishing bag

The **fishing bag** is a special bag for your fish:

- Right click with the bag in hand to open it
- Holds up to **45 fish**
- Displays total weight, count and average quality
- Fish you pick up will **automatically end up in the bag** if it is in your inventory
- You can take fish out of the bag and put it back in

## Fishing Trader

At **Angel-Trader** you can sell your fish for Pounds:

1. Open the fishing trader (NPC at spawn)
2. Place your fish or fishing bag in the GUI
3. Click **“Offer All”** to offer all fish from your inventory
4. Click **"Sell"** to sell everything at once

The price depends on the **weight** and **quality** of the fish. High-weight gourmet fish fetch the best prices.

## Angler's Handbook

The **Fishing Book** is your personal fish encyclopedia:

- Open with `/anglerbuch`
- Shows all fish species on the server
- Discovered (✓) vs. not yet caught (✗)
- A special sound sounds when a new fish species is caught for the first time

The fishing book is also accessible via the fishing trader (book symbol in the GUI).

## Fishing Enchantments

Special enchantments for your fishing rod:

| enchantment | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Rodpower** | Allows you to catch fish with higher requirements (rare species). Obtained by combining the correct sigil on the **Anvil**. |
| **Quality improvement** | Increases the likelihood of better fish quality |
| **Weight increase** | Fish become heavier → higher sales value |
