The lumberjack is the wood job: fell trees and earn job XP and Pounds for cutting down logs.

## How do you earn XP?

XP is available for **mining wood/logs** - the rarer the wood, the more:

| wood | Job XP |
|---|---|
| Oak | 1.89 |
| Birch | 1.65 |
| Spruce | 2.18 |
| tropical wood | 2.55 |
| Acacia | 2.69 |
| Mangrove | 2.9 |
| Black oak | 3.1 |
| Cherry / Pale Oak | 3.64 |

## ability

- **Timber** — falls an entire tree at once.

<div class="md-note">Self-placed blocks and logs felled with Timber do not give XP.</div>
