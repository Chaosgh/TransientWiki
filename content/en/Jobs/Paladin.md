# Paladin

The Paladin is the combat job: kill enemy monsters and bosses for job XP and Pounds.

## How do you earn XP?

| Opponent | Job XP |
|---|---|
| Zombie | 5.0 |
| Spider | 5.6 |
| Skeleton | 5.8 |
| Creepers | 6.4 |
| Blaze | 8.0 |
| Enderman | 16.0 |
| Wither | 180.0 |
| Warden | 220.0 |
| Ender Dragon | 350.0 |

<div class="md-note">Kills in the Citybuild world do not give job XP - go to the appropriate worlds to fight.</div>
