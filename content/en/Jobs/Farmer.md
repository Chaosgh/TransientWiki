# Farmer

The Farmer is the farming job: harvest mature crops for job XP and Pounds.

## How do you earn XP?

XP is only available for **fully grown** plants:

| crop | Job XP |
|---|---|
| Wheat | 2.3 |
| potatoes | 2.4 |
| Carrots | 2.6 |
| Pumpkin | 2.87 |
| Melon | 2.88 |
| nether wart | 2.92 |
| Beetroot | 3.12 |

## Skills

- **Replant** — automatically replants after harvest.
- **Netherite Hoe (right click)** — harvests and replants in one go.
- **Magnet** — collects the drops automatically.
