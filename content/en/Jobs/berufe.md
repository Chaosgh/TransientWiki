# Jobs & Professions

Choose a profession to earn Pounds and collect job XP. As the level increases, new possibilities become available.

You can have **all jobs active at the same time** — there is no limit.

## How do I see my job progress?

Use the command `/job` or `/berufe` to open the job menu. There you will see your level, your XP and the amount of Pounds required for the next level.
