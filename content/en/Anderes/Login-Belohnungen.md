For every day you log in you will receive a **daily login reward**. Rewards increase over **100 days** — the further you get through the calendar, the more **Pounds** and **ectoplasm** you get.

<div class="md-note">Don't confuse them with the <a href="/wiki/anderes/daily-quests">Daily Quests</a> - they are also available for completed tasks. You get the login reward just for logging in.</div>

## Here's how it works

- Every day when you log in you get **Pounds** — the amount continually increases.
- On **Milestone Days** (every 10 days) there are big bonuses: lots of Pounds, **ectoplasm** and special items.
- After day 100 the calendar is complete.

## Milestone Rewards

| day | Reward |
|---|---|
| 10 | 800 Pounds + 10 Ectoplasm |
| 20 | 1,600 Pounds + 25 ectoplasm |
| 30 | 4,600 Pounds + 50 Ectoplasm |
| 40 | 8,000 Pounds + 60 ectoplasm |
| 50 | 10,000 Pounds + 100 ectoplasm |
| 60 | 12,000 Pounds + 65 ectoplasm |
| 70 | 13,000 Pounds + 70 Ectoplasm + **Active Badge** (one-time) |
| 80 | 14,000 Pounds + 75 ectoplasm |
| 90 | 15,000 Pounds + 80 ectoplasm |
| 100 | 20,000 Pounds + 350 ectoplasm |

Between the milestones there are extras on individual days - e.g. Diamonds (Days 6 & 11), Auras (Day 55), a Potion of Activity (Day 89) and an Active Totem (Day 99).
