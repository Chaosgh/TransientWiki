Badges are **cosmetic badges** that float above your head — other players will see them around you.

## Command

| command | Aliases | Function |
|---|---|---|
| `/badges` | `/title`, `/titel` | Opens the badge menu |

## Here's how it works

- In the menu you can see all badges. You can equip the ones you have **authorization** for.
- **Click** on a badge = equip it. Click again = drop again.
- **Only one badge can be worn at a time**.
- Use the filter (funnel) to switch between “just your badges” and “all badges”.

## Receive badges

Badges are unlocked as **permissions** — for example through rewards (such as the **Active Badge** on [login day 70](/wiki/anderes/login-belohnungen)), promotions or the store.

## Visibility

- Your badge will be seen by **other players** in the same world near you (~300 blocks) - not by you.
- It floats as a badge above the head (not in the nameplate, tab or chat).
- No badge is displayed in **spectator mode**.
