# Advancements

Advancements are **Achievements** with **rewards** (Pounds, Ectoplasm or Items) that you unlock as you play. Open the in-game Advancement dashboard to see your progress and **claim** rewards.

## Categories

### Getting started
The first steps: arrive, open the wiki, use the spawn and resource world, open the job menu, gain your first job experience and collect your first reward.

### Work and crafts
Reach job levels **5** and **20**, work in three jobs (multi-talent), create a sigil and a rune, catch a special fish.

### Home and property
Enter your own and someone else's plot, own **5** and **10** plots, set a home, change plot flags and style, design with decoration.

### Trade and Economy
Check account balance, own **100,000** and **1,000,000** Pounds, send someone Pounds, buy from the shop, create your own shop, earn 10,000 Pounds through jobs.

### Crates and ectoplasm
Obtain first ectoplasm, open first crate, open 10 crates, draw a rare and a Mythos/Chronos reward.

### Equipment and Power
Use first ability, activate a passive ability, trigger a custom enchantment, use a rare ability.

### Fields & Animals · Forest & Wood · Underground
Collecting goals in the **Resource World**: plant and harvest, breed and milk animals, cut down trees, mine ores and diamonds and dig deep below Y=0.

### Community and cults
Open the cult overview, join a cult, write in the cult chat, achieve a cult promotion, use the weekly market.

## Hidden Advancements

Some achievements are **hidden** and only appear when you complete them - e.g. being online at 3 a.m., playing for 8 hours in a row, or the secret **cult founder** series (founding your own cult and expanding it with members and levels).

<div class="md-note">You have to actively collect rewards in the advancement overview.</div>
