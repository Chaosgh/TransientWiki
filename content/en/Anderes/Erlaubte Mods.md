# Allowed Mods

> This is the official whitelist. If it's not here, it's not allowed. Feel free to ask in Discord - if it's allowed, it'll be mentioned here.

---

## Clients (allowed)

The following complete clients are permitted. Individual integrated modules may **still be prohibited** and must remain disabled.

- **Labymod**
- **Badlion**
- **Lunar**
- **Feather**
- **Norisk**

---

## Graphics & Performance (allowed)

- **Optifine**
- **Sodium**
- **Shader packs** *(no X-Ray shaders)*
- **Dynamic Lights**
- **Distant Horizons**
- **Bobby**
- **Fullbright**

*Visual only. Anything that makes caves visible when they shouldn't be: forbidden.*

---

## UI & Quality of Life (allowed)

- **Inventory Organization Mods** — *No auto-assist. Sorting only.*
- **Minimaps** — *without cave scan.*
- **JEI / REI**
- **ShulkerBoxTooltip**
- **MapTooltip**
- **Armor and effect status indicators**
- **Pixel Hud**
- **Litematica** — *Display only. No auto-build, no printer functions.*
- **Do a Barrel Roll**
- **Moon Phase Display**

---

## Not allowed

So that no one claims that they cheated "accidentally":

- X-Ray of any kind
- Cave Finder/Ore Scanner
- Auto-fishing, auto-mining, auto-PvP, macro bots
- Anything that makes blocks visible that Minecraft says you shouldn't see
- Litematica Printer/Auto Placer
- Mod features of clients that violate these rules

---

## Submit new mods

Unsure? Report on Discord. Name, version, link, purpose. If it goes through, it will end up here.
