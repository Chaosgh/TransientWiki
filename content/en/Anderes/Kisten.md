## Possible rewards

Crates can contain the following things:

| Reward | Description |
|---|---|
| **pound** | Direct credit to your account |
| **Ectoplasm** | The server's premium currency |
| **Items** | Special Items |
| **Permissions** | Activation of features |
| **Badge** | Cosmetic Badges |

## Ectoplasm

**Ectoplasm** is the server's premium currency:

- Available via **[Store](https://store.transientrealm.de)** (Real Money)
- Rarely earned through **gameplay**
- Mainly used to open **Elemental, Chronos and Mythos Crates**
- Can be **transferred** between players

## Open crates

You open crates at the **Crate Area (Distributor)** at spawn. Open the menu there and select the crate - assuming you have the necessary credit (Votecoins for the Vote Crate, Ectoplasm for Elemental, Chronos and Mythos Crates). A reel animation shows your reward.

You can shorten the animation using the **Skip button** if you want to see the result quickly.
