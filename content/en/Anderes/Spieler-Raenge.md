Server ranks show your status in the chat and tab list and provide additional benefits depending on your rank.

## The ranks

| Rank | How to get it |
|---|---|
| **Apprentice** | Starting rank — every player automatically has |
| **Scholar** | About [Store](https://store.transientrealm.de) |
| **Seer** | About Store |
| **Celestial** | About Store |
| **Dreamer** | About Store |
| **Legend** | About Store |

## How do I level up?

**Apprentice** is the starting rank that everyone has from the start. The higher ranks (from **Scholar**) are available **via the store**: [store.transientrealm.de](https://store.transientrealm.de). They will be assigned directly to your account.

<div class="md-note">The scholar rank is occasionally given away - then for a limited time.</div>

<div class="md-note"> Do not confuse with your <strong> job level </strong> (rises above <a href="/wiki/jobs/berufe"> job experience </a>) and the <strong> cult ranks </strong> (<a href="/wiki/kult/raenge"> clan internal </a>) — these are separate systems. </div>
