# currencies

On TransientRealm there are three currencies with clearly separated roles.

## Pounds

The **primary in-game currency**. Earn Pounds through [Jobs](/wiki/jobs/berufe), [Chest Shops](/wiki/anderes/kistenshops), [Daily Quests](/wiki/anderes/daily-quests) and [Login Rewards](/wiki/anderes/login-belohnungen). Use them for shops, trading and [Warps](/wiki/anderes/warps).

## Ectoplasm

The **premium or secondary currency**. Receive via daily milestones, events and the store. Mainly used to open **Elemental, Chronos and Mythos [Crates](/wiki/anderes/kisten)**. Transferable between players.

## Rubies

The **cult currency**. Cults receive Rubies when members donate Pounds to the cult fund (`/kult donate`). They are used for [Cult Level Ups](/wiki/kult/levelsystem) and [Faction Changes](/wiki/kult/fraktionen) (25,000 Rubies).

<div class="md-note"><strong>Job progress</strong> does not run on a currency: As you work, you collect <a href="/wiki/jobs/berufe">Job experience (XP)</a> and thus move up in job level.</div>
