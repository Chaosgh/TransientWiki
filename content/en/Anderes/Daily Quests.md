# Daily Quests

Talk to **Simon**, an NPC at `/spawn`, to view your daily quests, or open them from anywhere with `/quests`.

Every day, each player will receive **three new Daily Quests** that can yield **Pounds** and **Ectoplasm**. At **0:00 a.m.** all daily tasks will be automatically reset and redistributed.

There are different types of quests that reward different play styles:

## Mining Quests

Mine a certain number of specific blocks.

## Fishing Quests

Catch a predetermined amount of custom fish using your own fishing system.

## Crafting Quests

Craft certain items in the crafting table.

## Combat Quests

Defeat a given number of mobs.

Anyone who completes all daily quests will receive valuable rewards and daily variety.
