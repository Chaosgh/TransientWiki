Deep in the Transient Maze, treasures, traps and secrets await — if you manage to get out alive.

## Enter

Speak to the **NPC at spawn** to be teleported into the labyrinth. You will land at a **random location** in the Maze.

<div class="md-note">
If you die in the labyrinth, there is a <strong>5-minute cooldown</strong> before you can re-enter. Your remaining cooldown is shown when you try to enter.
</div>

## Illusory walls

Not every wall is real. Some walls are **illusions** — you can just walk through them. Keep an eye out for suspicious spots in the Maze.

## Safes & Maze Keys

There are locked **safes** in the labyrinth. To open it you need a **Maze Key**:

> *"Forged from the Core of Change. Opens ancient vaults."*

Maze keys can be found as loot in the Maze itself. Vaults contain valuable rewards.

## Poison aura

The **Poison Aura** drops in the labyrinth - a rare item that the **Sigil Magician** needs to create a **Poison Sigil**.

> *“A Mystical Origin of Betrayal.”*

## Tips

- Flying is **not allowed** in the labyrinth
- Explore carefully — traps are often well hidden
- Don't go alone if you're not sure
- Bring enough equipment and food
- Watch out for suspicious walls — some are permeable
