# Beginner's Guide to TransientRealm

This guide explains the first steps on **TransientRealm**, a German Minecraft server with **Citybuild**, **Jobs**, **Quests**, **Economy** and other proprietary game systems.

If you are new to the server, you should follow this order:

---

## Join the server

TransientRealm is a pure **Java edition** server — Bedrock (mobile, console, Windows Store version) is not supported.

<div class="md-note"><strong>Server address:</strong> <code>transientrealm.de</code> · <strong>Version:</strong> Minecraft Java 26.1.2</div>

1. Start Minecraft **Java Edition** in version **26.1.2**.
2. Open **Multiplayer** → **Add Server**.
3. Enter `transientrealm.de` as the server address, save and connect.

The first time you join, you arrive directly at spawn. Start exploring from there.

---

## 1. Understanding spawn

At the spawn you will find the most important starting points for your entry:

- NPCs for daily quests
- Access to crates and rewards
- Merchants and system NPCs
- Notes on the resource world, Citybuild and other features

The spawn is deliberately set up as a landmark. Run it through completely before you focus on individual systems.

---

## 2. Get your own plot in the Citybuild

For most players, everyday life begins in **Citybuild**. There you secure a plot of land and can start building, storing and planning straight away.

```text
/plot auto
```

Then you have your own plot and a stable base for farms, shops and trade.

More about this: [Citybuild](/wiki/anderes/citybuild)

---

## 3. Choose a job

The **Jobs system** is one of the most important parts of the server. Through jobs you earn **Pounds**, collect progress and unlock new opportunities in the long term.

```text
/berufe
```

A sensible start:

- Resource focus: prospectors, farmers, fellers
- Combat and loot: Paladin, butcher
- Exploration and special systems: explorers, magnet fishers
- Crafting and refining: Rune mechanic, sigil mage

More about this: [Jobs & Professions](/wiki/jobs/berufe)

---

## 4. Complete your daily quests

**Daily Quests** are ideal for reliably receiving rewards every day. They help you with early progress and complement jobs, farming and trading very well.

More about this: [Daily Quests](/wiki/anderes/daily-quests)

---

## 5. Gather resources in the Resource World

The **Resource World** is intended for the targeted collection of raw materials. This keeps your Citybuild plot clean while you can farm ores, wood and other materials efficiently.

```text
/farmwelt
```

More about this: [Resource World](/wiki/anderes/farmwelt)

---

## 6. Understanding currencies

There are multiple currencies with different roles on TransientRealm:

| Currency | Typical sources | Typical usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pounds | Jobs, Quests, Trade | Main currency for everyday life |
| Rubies | Cult Internal Systems | Progression and group systems |
| Ectoplasm | Shop or special rewards | Premium Crates and Special Content |

If you understand the currencies early on, you will get to grips with the economy of the server much more quickly.

---

## 7. Explore advanced systems

Once the basics are in place, the more complex systems are worthwhile:

- [Sigil Mage](/wiki/jobs/siegelmagier) for sigils, refinements and magical item systems
- [Rune Mechanic](/wiki/jobs/runenmechaniker) for runes and server crafting
- [Researcher](/wiki/jobs/forscher) for artifacts, excavating and rare finds
- [Cults](/wiki/kult/kulte) for group play, economy and progression
- [Crates](/wiki/anderes/kisten) for rewards and progression systems

---

## The most important commands to get started

| command | Function |
| --- | --- |
| `/spawn` | Back to spawn |
| `/berufe` | Open jobs menu |
| `/plot auto` | Automatically claim a plot |
| `/plot home` | Teleport to your own plot |
| `/farmwelt` | Switch to Resource World |

If you still have questions after the basics, the [FAQ](/wiki/faq) will help you.
